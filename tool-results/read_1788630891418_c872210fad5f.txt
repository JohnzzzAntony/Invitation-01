     1→# Online RSVP — High-Fidelity Product Wireframe & Full Build Specification
     2→
     3→## 1. Product Overview
     4→
     5→Build a modern, production-ready RSVP/event website SaaS inspired by the feature set of Online-RSVP.
     6→
     7→The platform should combine:
     8→
     9→- Event website builder
    10→- Invitation builder
    11→- RSVP management
    12→- Guest CRM
    13→- Guest groups
    14→- Sub-events
    15→- Email invitations and reminders
    16→- QR-code RSVP
    17→- Event analytics
    18→- Gallery
    19→- Accommodation
    20→- Registry/donations
    21→- Payments
    22→- Custom domains
    23→- Template marketplace
    24→- Admin/SaaS management
    25→
    26→The product should feel like a combination of:
    27→
    28→- Canva
    29→- Shopify Theme Editor
    30→- Framer
    31→- Eventbrite
    32→
    33→but with a much simpler event-creation workflow.
    34→
