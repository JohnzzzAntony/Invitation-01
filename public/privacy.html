Privacy Policy

Invitara is built so that there is very little of your data for anyone to mishandle. Your event content lives in your own browser, not on our servers. This page explains exactly what that means.

1. The short version

No accounts. No database. No tracking. Everything you type into the builder — names, dates, venue, photos, colours — is saved in your browser's localStorage on your own device. We never receive it, so we cannot read it, sell it or lose it. Card details are never written to storage of any kind.

The trade-off is worth stating plainly: because your event lives on your device, clearing your browser data or switching to a different browser or computer will lose it. See Retention & deletion.

2. Who we are

Invitara provides event-website templates with online RSVP collection. For the purposes of the UK GDPR and EU GDPR, we are the data controller for the limited information described below. You can reach us at hello@your-domain.example.

3. What we handle

DataWhyWhere it goes

We do not ask for, and have no way to collect, your address, phone number, date of birth, or any special-category data.

4. Where your data lives

The builder writes to a small number of localStorage keys on the device you are using. These are not cookies: they are never transmitted with requests, and no server — ours or anyone else's — can read them.

  • ever-rsvp-event — the full content and design of your event site
  • ever-rsvp-selected-design — the template you chose
  • ever-rsvp-custom-tpl — any designs you created yourself
  • ever-rsvp-flow — whether checkout has been completed

You can inspect or erase all of them at any time from your browser's developer tools, or by clearing site data for this domain.

5. Payments

Invitara is paid for once per invitation — the design’s price plus your chosen plan, from AED 29. There is no subscription and no recurring charge.

Card details are entered into the checkout form, validated in your browser, and handed to our payment provider. They are never written to localStorage, never placed in a cookie, and never stored on our systems. Only the resulting order number is kept, in your browser, so the builder knows your purchase went through. Our payment provider processes the transaction under its own privacy policy and is responsible for card data under PCI DSS.

6. Your guests' replies

When your guests RSVP, they provide their name, attendance, party size and any answers to questions you added. That information is yours: you are the controller of it, and you are responsible for telling your guests how you will use it and for keeping it no longer than you need.

We act only as a processor for guest replies, handling them on your instructions in order to show them back to you. We do not use guest data for marketing, do not share it, and do not build profiles from it.

7. Third parties

Invitara loads a deliberately small number of external resources:

ServicePurposeWhat it can see
Google Fonts The typefaces used across the site Your IP address and browser, when a font file is fetched
OpenStreetMap The optional venue map embed on your event page Your IP address, only if a map is shown
Payment provider Processing your one-time payment Your card details and billing information

There are no analytics scripts, advertising pixels, session recorders, chat widgets or social-media trackers on this site. Our Content Security Policy blocks third-party scripts outright, so none can be added silently.

8. Cookies & tracking

Invitara sets no cookies at all — which is why you have not been shown a cookie banner. We use localStorage, described above, purely to make the product work; it is strictly necessary storage and carries no identifier that could follow you between sites.

9. Your rights

Under the UK/EU GDPR and comparable laws you have the right to access, correct, erase, restrict, port and object to the processing of your personal data. For nearly everything Invitara holds, you can exercise those rights directly and immediately: the data is on your device, and clearing your site data erases it completely.

For anything we do hold — an e-mail you sent us, a newsletter subscription, a payment record — write to hello@your-domain.example and we will respond within 30 days. If you are unhappy with our response you may complain to your local supervisory authority (in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office).

10. Retention & deletion

  • Event content: kept in your browser until you clear it. We cannot delete it for you, because we never had a copy. Equally, we cannot restore it — export or back up anything you would be sorry to lose.
  • Payment records: retained by our payment provider for as long as tax and anti-fraud law requires, typically six to seven years.
  • Support e-mail: kept for up to two years, then deleted.
  • Newsletter: kept until you unsubscribe, which every e-mail lets you do in one click.

11. Children

Invitara is not directed at children and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 16. Events such as christenings and children's parties are of course welcome — but the account holder must be an adult.

12. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of the page. Material changes will be announced on the site before they take effect.

13. Contact

Questions about privacy, or a request about your data: hello@your-domain.example. See also our Terms of Service.
